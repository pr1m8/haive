Even More Fascinating AI Agent Games

Time & Memory Games

Temporal Perspective Shifting

Core Concept: Agents embody the same identity at different life stages, maintaining coherent character while demonstrating how perspectives evolve across time.

From a Human Perspective: This offers profound insight into life's developmental arc—how we remain ourselves while fundamentally changing. It helps us understand both older and younger generations by embodying their temporal perspectives. It might foster compassion for our past and future selves while illuminating the continuity beneath life changes.

From an LLM Perspective: This requires modeling how core personality structures persist while perspectives, priorities, and cognitive patterns evolve over time. We must represent both the stable and changing aspects of identity across developmental stages.

Game Mechanics: - Detailed character profiles with core traits and values - Agents embody the same person at different ages (child, young adult, middle age, elder) - Face consistent life scenarios from each temporal perspective - Success measured by both coherence across ages and authentic developmental shifts

Memory Palace Architects

Core Concept: Agents design symbolic memory structures to encode complex knowledge, then test their effectiveness for learning and recall.

From a Human Perspective: This revitalizes ancient memory techniques for the information age, offering practical tools for managing complexity. It makes abstract memory processes tangible and manipulable. It might help us develop more effective approaches to learning while appreciating the artistic dimensions of memory itself.

From an LLM Perspective: This explores the intersection of spatial reasoning, symbolism, and memory encoding. We must translate abstract information into concrete spatial and symbolic representations that leverage human cognitive strengths.

Game Mechanics: - Complex knowledge domains are selected for memorization - Agents design detailed memory palaces with symbolic representations - Other agents attempt to use these structures for learning and recall - Success measured by memorization efficiency, recall accuracy, and intuitive design

Alternate Timeline Weaver

Core Concept: Agents maintain consistent personal identities while developing life narratives through drastically different historical circumstances and choices.

From a Human Perspective: This illuminates how our identities are shaped by both inherent tendencies and circumstances. It helps us distinguish which aspects of ourselves are core versus contingent. It satisfies our curiosity about "roads not taken" in our own lives while deepening appreciation for life's complexity.

From an LLM Perspective: This requires balancing personality consistency with realistic adaptation to different circumstances. We must model how core traits express themselves differently in various environments while maintaining psychological plausibility.

Game Mechanics: - Core character profiles with stable traits and values - Multiple life trajectories developed through different eras or decisions - Must maintain psychological continuity while showing appropriate adaptation - Success measured by both consistency and believable divergence

Why These New Games Matter

For Humans

  1. Reflective Understanding - They reveal patterns in cognition, culture, and relationship that are typically invisible to us but shape our lives.

  2. Practical Application - Many offer approaches that could enhance decision-making, creativity, communication, and conflict resolution.

  3. Conceptual Expansion - They stretch our imagination about possible ways of thinking, relating, and organizing beyond familiar patterns.

  4. Integration Opportunities - They suggest ways to integrate different domains often kept separate: cognition and culture, emotion and decision-making, individual and collective.

  5. Hopeful Futures - They demonstrate possibilities for positive human development both individually and collectively.

For LLMs

  1. System Dynamics - They require modeling complex adaptive systems with multiple interacting components and feedback loops.

  2. Mental Model Mapping - They demand sophisticated representation of different ways minds can structure experience and process information.

  3. Cross-Domain Integration - They test ability to identify meaningful connections between disparate knowledge domains.

  4. Process Representation - They explore dynamic processes over time rather than static knowledge.

  5. Metacognition - They enhance understanding of thinking itself—both human and artificial—across different architectures.

Implementation Priorities

The most valuable implementation approaches would:

  1. Start Simple but Profound - Begin with games that require minimal technical infrastructure but address fundamental questions

  2. Enable Genuine Emergence - Create conditions where unexpected patterns and insights can emerge rather than being predetermined

  3. Capture Process Data - Document not just outcomes but the developmental trajectories that produce them

  4. Support Iteration - Allow continuous refinement based on insights gained from early implementations

  5. Maintain Human Connection - Ensure games remain relevant to human concerns while exploring new possibilities

  6. Build Interoperability - Create components that can be combined across different games to enable novel hybrid forms

With these priorities in mind, these games could generate significant value through both their process and their outcomes—deepening our understanding of intelligence, creativity, and human potential while suggesting practical approaches to enhancing these qualities.
