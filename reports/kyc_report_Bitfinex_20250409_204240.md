# Regulatory Compliance and Licensing

## Key Findings

As part of our regulatory compliance assessment, we examined Bitfinex's adherence to local and international regulatory requirements. Our investigation revealed several critical developments:

- **UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Warning**: Bitfinex is listed on the FCA's warning list as an unauthorized firm (source: [FCA Statement](https://blog.bitfinex.com/announcements/fca-statement-2/), 29 October 2023). Despite proactive measures to comply with FCA requirements, Bitfinex's status remains unchanged.

- **Operational Adjustments in the UK**: To align with regulatory demands, particularly from the FCA, Bitfinex has ceased offering certain products and services, such as individual account verification, staking, and lending for UK-based customers who joined after 1 November 2023 (source: [Notice to UK Customers](https://blog.bitfinex.com/announcements/notice-to-uk-customers/), 4 January 2023). This indicates Bitfinex's attempt to mitigate potential regulatory breaches.

- **Licensing in El Salvador**: Contrastingly, Bitfinex Securities has secured a license as a Digital Asset Service Provider under El Salvador's Digital Asset Issuance Law, illustrating compliance and operational legitimacy within this jurisdiction (source: [El Salvador Licensing](https://blog.bitfinex.com/bitfinex-securities/bitfinex-securities-launches-express-onboarding-for-salvadoran-residents/), April 2023).

2. **Restricted Operations for UK Customers**: Restricting services for UK-based customers may indicate either attempts to circumvent regulatory requirements or challenges in meeting these requirements in full.
3. **Operational and Compliance Scope**: While licensed in El Salvador, Bitfinex's compliance profile varies significantly between jurisdictions, raising concerns about consistent adherence to regulatory expectations globally.

# AML/KYC Practices: Bitfinex

## Key Findings from Research
Bitfinex operates as a cryptocurrency exchange platform, adhering to certain AML and KYC practices. According to information from **GetID** [source](https://getid.com/aml-kyc-crypto-exchanges-wallets/), Bitfinex has a unique approach to KYC requirements, distinguishing between crypto-only users and users handling fiat currencies. Users who strictly engage in crypto transactions are not required to undergo KYC verification processes. However, users who wish to deposit or trade fiat currencies must provide comprehensive identification, including an address, phone number, proof of address, and two forms of government-issued identification. This approach raises considerations about their alignment with traditional financial institutions' compliance requirements.

The platform's practices need to be evaluated in the context of evolving regulatory frameworks, such as the CANSEE Act introduced by Sen. Jack Reed [source](https://blog.bitfinex.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Bitfinex-Alpha-64.pdf), which emphasizes stricter AML and KYC regulations for decentralized finance platforms.

## Identified Risk Factors
1. **Differentiated KYC Practices**: Bitfinex's policy of allowing crypto-only users to bypass KYC processes presents risks related to anonymity, which could potentially facilitate money laundering or other illicit activities.
   
2. **Regulatory Changes**: With ongoing legislative developments like the CANSEE Act, there is potential exposure to increased regulatory scrutiny if Bitfinex's practices are perceived not to align with the tightened KYC and AML requirements.

3. **Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) Classification**: Bitfinex inherently faces the typical risks associated with VASPs, including security of funds, transparency in operations, and adherence to international standards.

## Relevance to Company's Risk Appetite
Corpay's risk appetite statement clearly articulates a disinterest in engaging with anonymous, unregistered, or unregulated virtual currency exchanges, prioritizing transparency and compliance. Bitfinex's practice of allowing anonymity for crypto-only users might conflict with Corpay’s prohibitions against such entities. Therefore, handling business transactions with Bitfinex could fall outside the scope of Corpay’s appetite, especially considering stringent requirements for identifying and verifying customer identities.

## Supporting Evidence and Sources
- **GetID**: Highlights the distinctive approach of Bitfinex in handling KYC procedures, especially regarding the segregation of crypto-only and fiat-supported transactions.
- **Bitfinex Blog**: Provides insights into the legislative environment, including the CANSEE Act, that could impact Bitfinex's operations in the DeFi sector.

## Conclusion
Bitfinex's AML/KYC practices present distinct risk factors due to their differentiated approach towards identity verification based on transaction types, especially in light of regulatory advancements requiring stronger oversight. For Corpay, engaging with Bitfinex may propose challenges, primarily due to potential incompatibilities with its risk appetite framework, focusing on transparent operations and compliance with financial crime regulations. It is essential for Corpay to conduct enhanced due diligence to ensure alignment with its compliance standard and regulatory necessities when considering business interactions with entities in the cryptocurrency sector.

## Technical and Cybersecurity Risk Assessment: Bitfinex

### Key Findings from Research

Bitfinex has made significant advancements in its cybersecurity measures, especially following previous security breaches. In 2023, Bitfinex successfully recovered approximately $315,000 of stolen assets with the assistance of the Department of Homeland Security [source](https://www.cryptoninjas.net/exchange/bitfinex-review/). This collaboration indicates Bitfinex's proactive approach to addressing past incidents and compliance with governmental agencies.

Furthermore, Bitfinex has completed its SOC 2 Type 1 compliance and is committed to ongoing assessments to achieve SOC 2 Type 2 compliance [source](https://blog.bitfinex.com/announcements/bitfinex-has-completed-the-soc-2-type-1-compliance/). The SOC 2 reports evaluate data security control effectiveness on a continuous basis, underscoring Bitfinex's focus on robust security controls, including the implementation of firewalls, intrusion detection systems, DDoS protection, and regular security audits.

Bitfinex employs a multi-pronged approach to cybersecurity, featuring protection mechanisms such as cold storage solutions and multisignature protocols for asset security, with 99.5% of user funds secured offline [source](https://news.zke.com/enhancing-security-for-bitfinex-users-comprehensive-checklist-released/). Advanced user security measures, including two-factor authentication (2FA) and withdrawal protection through whitelisting, further enhance security [source](https://news.zke.com/bitfinex-highlights-enhanced-security-measures-for-crypto-traders/).

### Identified Risk Factors

Despite these improvements, Bitfinex operates as a virtual asset service provider, a sector that inherently carries higher cyber risks due to the nature of digital currencies and exchanges. The historical breach of Bitfinex in 2016, which involved a substantial loss of funds, highlights the residual risks in handling cybersecurity breaches [source](https://www.cryptoninjas.net/exchange/bitfinex-review/). Although measures have been taken to mitigate this risk, it remains a pertinent factor in assessing Bitfinex's cybersecurity profile.

Additionally, while they offer sophisticated security features, no platform is entirely immune to cyber threats, necessitating continuous vigilance and adaptation of security practices [source](https://www.vpnsuper.com/blog/is-bitfinex-safe).

### Relevance to Company's Risk Appetite

Corpay’s risk appetite statement details a cautious approach toward virtual asset service providers due to the associated risks of unlicensed or unregulated activities. Bitfinex’s operations necessitate enhanced diligence due to its classification in this category. However, Bitfinex's demonstrated compliance through rigorous security standards and cooperation with regulatory bodies aligns with Corpay's requirements for engaging reputable and compliant businesses.

Given the comprehensive cybersecurity infrastructure Bitfinex has employed, their alignment with formal compliance standards provides assurance. However, ongoing scrutiny and proactive measures are essential to mitigate potential vulnerabilities inherent in the crypto exchange industry.
