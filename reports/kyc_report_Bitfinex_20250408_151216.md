# AML/KYC Practices Assessment

## Key Findings

Bitfinex implements distinctive AML and KYC practices that differentiate between cryptocurrency and fiat transactions. Users who interact solely with cryptocurrencies on Bitfinex do not undergo traditional KYC procedures, thereby allowing for anonymity within those transactions. Conversely, users who engage in fiat currency transactions are required to provide comprehensive identification details, including proof of address and two forms of government-issued ID ([source](https://getid.com/aml-kyc-crypto-exchanges-wallets/)).

## Identified Risk Factors

1. **Lack of KYC for Crypto Transactions**: The absence of KYC processes for crypto-to-crypto transactions raises a significant concern regarding anonymity and potential misuse for illicit activities, such as money laundering or the financing of terror.
2. **Differentiated Onboarding Process**: The distinction between KYC requirements based on transaction type may pose challenges in consistently identifying and monitoring risky customer behaviors across the platform.

## Relevance to Company's Risk Appetite

Corpay's risk appetite statement explicitly indicates a lack of tolerance for unregulated virtual currency exchanges, which presents challenges regarding Bitfinex’s business model. The allowance for cryptocurrency transactions without KYC aligns with the type of business activities Corpay seeks to restrict, evidencing a potential misalignment with Corpay’s prohibition on engaging with anonymous numbered accounts and virtual currencies through unregulated exchanges.

Additionally, the differentiated approach to KYC poses a risk that Bitfinex may indirectly facilitate illegal activities or facilitate unverified transactions, directly clashing with Corpay's heightened concerns over cash-intensive business models and virtual asset service providers.

## Supporting Evidence and Sources

- Bitfinex permits users engaging in crypto transactions to operate without completing KYC, which is substantiated in the context of cryptocurrency exchanges and wallets evolving to include KYC within their AML programs ([source](https://getid.com/aml-kyc-crypto-exchanges-wallets/)).
- Corpay’s risk appetite clearly restricts activities related to anonymous accounts and unregulated virtual currencies, pertinent to Bitfinex’s operational model not requiring identity verification for crypto users.

## Conclusion

Bitfinex’s existing AML/KYC framework, specifically regarding crypto-only transactions, presents substantial risks under Corpay's risk appetite framework. The operating model does not fully align with Corpay's requirements for regulated account practice, posing concerns about the platform’s exposure to illicit activities. The disparity between KYC measures for different transaction types within Bitfinex should prompt careful consideration and potential reevaluation of partnership or engagement terms in accordance with Corpay's stringent compliance standards.

# Technical and Cybersecurity Risk Assessment for Bitfinex

## Key Findings

Bitfinex, a cryptocurrency exchange operational since 2012, employs comprehensive security measures to safeguard its platform and user assets. These measures include the use of firewalls, intrusion detection, two-factor authentication (2FA), and encryption to protect user data, as demonstrated in its completion of SOC 2 Type 1 compliance (source: [Blog Bitfinex](https://blog.bitfinex.com/announcements/bitfinex-has-completed-the-soc-2-type-1-compliance/)). Beyond basic security certifications, Bitfinex adopts sophisticated protections such as intelligent DDoS defenses, AI-driven fraud monitoring, and regular security audits to bolster its resilience against cyber threats (source: [ZKE News](https://news.zke.com/enhancing-security-for-bitfinex-users-comprehensive-checklist-released/)).

Notably, Bitfinex stores approximately 99.5% of user funds in offline, multisignature wallets, minimizing the risk from online threats, while only 0.5% are kept in hot wallets for daily operations. This best practice reduces the likelihood of cyber attacks compromising large volumes of assets (source: [ZKE News](https://news.zke.com/enhancing-security-for-bitfinex-users-comprehensive-checklist-released/)). The two-factor authentication required for logins, API key creation, and withdrawals ensures an additional layer of security (source: [VPN Super](https://www.vpnsuper.com/blog/is-bitfinex-safe)).

## Identified Risk Factors

Despite robust security measures, Bitfinex is not entirely immune to risks. Their implication in past security breaches, including a significant cryptocurrency heist resulting in a recovery of over $10 billion by the US government, highlights vulnerabilities related to operational security (source: [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/bitfinex-hacker-gets-5-years-for-10-billion-bitcoin-heist/)). Such instances indicate potential systemic risks that may directly impact technical and cybersecurity infrastructure.

The continued existence of threat vectors associated with cryptocurrency exchanges—such as phishing attacks and unauthorized access—underscores the need for constant vigilance and improvement in security frameworks.

## Relevance to Company's Risk Appetite

According to Corpay's Risk Appetite Statement, the company exercises caution with virtual asset service providers, requiring heightened due diligence. Bitfinex, as a virtual asset service provider, falls within the category of entities subject to vigorous scrutiny under Corpay’s guidelines. While Bitfinex demonstrates compliance with regulatory standards and proactive security investments, its history of being a target for significant criminal acts warrants detailed evaluation to ensure alignment with Corpay’s risk thresholds for client engagements.
