# Business Activities Analysis

# AML/KYC Practices: Bitfinex

## Key Findings from Research
Bitfinex operates as a cryptocurrency exchange platform, adhering to certain AML and KYC practices. According to information from **GetID** [source](https://getid.com/aml-kyc-crypto-exchanges-wallets/), Bitfinex has a unique approach to KYC requirements, distinguishing between crypto-only users and users handling fiat currencies. Users who strictly engage in crypto transactions are not required to undergo KYC verification processes. However, users who wish to deposit or trade fiat currencies must provide comprehensive identification, including an address, phone number, proof of address, and two forms of government-issued identification. This approach raises considerations about their alignment with traditional financial institutions' compliance requirements.

The platform's practices need to be evaluated in the context of evolving regulatory frameworks, such as the CANSEE Act introduced by Sen. Jack Reed [source](https://blog.bitfinex.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Bitfinex-Alpha-64.pdf), which emphasizes stricter AML and KYC regulations for decentralized finance platforms.

## Identified Risk Factors
1. **Differentiated KYC Practices**: Bitfinex's policy of allowing crypto-only users to bypass KYC processes presents risks related to anonymity, which could potentially facilitate money laundering or other illicit activities.
   
2. **Regulatory Changes**: With ongoing legislative developments like the CANSEE Act, there is potential exposure to increased regulatory scrutiny if Bitfinex's practices are perceived not to align with the tightened KYC and AML requirements.

3. **Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) Classification**: Bitfinex inherently faces the typical risks associated with VASPs, including security of funds, transparency in operations, and adherence to international standards.

## Relevance to Company's Risk Appetite
Corpay's risk appetite statement clearly articulates a disinterest in engaging with anonymous, unregistered, or unregulated virtual currency exchanges, prioritizing transparency and compliance. Bitfinex's practice of allowing anonymity for crypto-only users might conflict with Corpay’s prohibitions against such entities. Therefore, handling business transactions with Bitfinex could fall outside the scope of Corpay’s appetite, especially considering stringent requirements for identifying and verifying customer identities.

## Supporting Evidence and Sources
- **GetID**: Highlights the distinctive approach of Bitfinex in handling KYC procedures, especially regarding the segregation of crypto-only and fiat-supported transactions.
- **Bitfinex Blog**: Provides insights into the legislative environment, including the CANSEE Act, that could impact Bitfinex's operations in the DeFi sector.

## Conclusion
Bitfinex's AML/KYC practices present distinct risk factors due to their differentiated approach towards identity verification based on transaction types, especially in light of regulatory advancements requiring stronger oversight. For Corpay, engaging with Bitfinex may propose challenges, primarily due to potential incompatibilities with its risk appetite framework, focusing on transparent operations and compliance with financial crime regulations. It is essential for Corpay to conduct enhanced due diligence to ensure alignment with its compliance standard and regulatory necessities when considering business interactions with entities in the cryptocurrency sector.
